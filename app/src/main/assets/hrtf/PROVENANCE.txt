MIT KEMAR normal pinna HRIR measurements. Copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory.
Authors: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, MIT Media Lab Machine Listening Group.
Citation: Gardner, W. G. and Martin, K. D. (1995), HRTF measurements of a KEMAR,
Journal of the Acoustical Society of America 97(6), 3907-3908.
License: accompanying MIT_KEMAR_LICENSE.txt permits use with author citation.
SOFA metadata contains a generic "No license provided" string; the explicit
accompanying MIT_KEMAR_pinnae license identifies this dataset and states the terms above.
Source mirror: https://github.com/hoene/libmysofa/blob/648eed03472e6720a1ea45d1a1f86c4efb569ff9/share/MIT_KEMAR_normal_pinna.sofa
License mirror: https://github.com/hoene/libmysofa/blob/648eed03472e6720a1ea45d1a1f86c4efb569ff9/tests/LICENSE.MIT_KEMAR_pinnae
Extraction: tools/extract_hrtf.py; 0-degree elevation, 1.4m measurement radius,
8 azimuths in 45-degree steps, 44100Hz, full 512 taps per ear, Data.Delay=0.
SOFA azimuth: 0 front, +90 left, 180 back, 270 right. Receiver0 left, receiver1 right.
No time alignment, per-ear scaling, or minimum-phase conversion was applied.
Virtual distance in app scales repetition/gain only, not near-field HRTF or reverb.
